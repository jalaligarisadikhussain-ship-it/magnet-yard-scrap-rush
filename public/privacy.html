Magnet Yard: Scrap Rush — Privacy

Last revised: October 2026. Draft for developer review before public publishing.

This browser game is playable without creating an account. The standalone build does not send a player profile, contact details, or payment data to a developer-operated backend.

Data saved on your device

To keep upgrades and progression between sessions, the game saves coins, drone upgrade levels, contract progress, completed shifts, personal best and graphics quality to your browser's local storage under magnet-yard-scrap-rush-save-v1. You may clear these data using your browser's site-data settings. There is currently no in-game cloud sync.

Third-party platforms

If you play through a hosting or game-distribution platform, that platform may independently handle advertising, analytics, cookies, sign-in and other technical information. Refer to the hosting platform's own privacy policy for its processing. A CrazyGames SDK integration is conditionally initialized only when the game runs in that platform's environment; current release approval is not implied.

External content

The presentation may load licensed fonts from Google Fonts, depending on the build and network settings. Those requests are handled according to the external provider's policies. No third-party game models, textures or recorded audio are required.
