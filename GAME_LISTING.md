# MAGNET YARD: SCRAP RUSH — Game Listing Draft

Title: Magnet Yard: Scrap Rush
Genre: Single-player 3D arcade / magnetic salvage action
Engine: HTML5 / WebGL2 / Three.js
Game shift: 90 seconds
Saving: Browser localStorage, no account required

## Short description
Pilot a magnetic drone, capture valuable scrap, dodge crushers and bank cargo to unlock upgrades before your 90-second shift ends.

## Full description
Fly across a neon industrial scrapyard in an original magnetic drone. Attract steel, copper and rare alloy into visible orbit, race back to the recycler for coins, avoid dangerous crushers, and balance cargo weight against movement speed. Upgrade magnet reach, cargo capacity and drone handling. Trigger Magnet Surge, complete contracts, chase timed golden cores and priority deliveries, and bank valuable loads in the final 20-second Sprint for extra coins. Beat your personal best and build better tech across shifts.

## Controls
Desktop: WASD or Arrow keys, click to move, Space for Magnet Surge. Pause and Mute on the HUD.
Mobile: Touch joystick, on-screen Surge, Pause, Mute and Upgrades.
Saving: localStorage preserves coins, upgrades, contracts, completed shifts and best score.
